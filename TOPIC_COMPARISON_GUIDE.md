# Topic Intelligence Comparison Guide

## Overview

The Topic Intelligence page now includes powerful comparison capabilities that allow Dayforce executives to compare customer feedback topics between Dayforce and any competitor. This feature provides strategic insights for competitive positioning, gap analysis, and identifying unique strengths.

## How to Use

### 1. **Select Primary Focus**
- Choose which product to analyze (defaults to Dayforce)
- Options: All Products, Dayforce, SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, UKG Pro, ADP Workforce Now

### 2. **Enable Comparison Mode**
- Check the "📊 Enable Comparison" checkbox
- This activates the competitor comparison interface

### 3. **Select Competitor**
- Choose which competitor to compare against
- The dropdown excludes the primary focus product

### 4. **View Metrics**
- **Focus Product Reviews**: Number of reviews for your primary selection
- **Competitor Reviews**: Number of reviews for the comparison product
- **Topic Overlap**: Percentage of topics that appear in both products (helps understand market convergence)

## What You'll See in Comparison Mode

### **Topic Categorization**

The analysis automatically categorizes topics into three groups:

#### 🎯 Primary Product Only Topics
- Topics mentioned exclusively in your primary product's reviews
- Shows what customers discuss uniquely about this product
- Indicates unique features, strengths, or pain points

**Strategic Value:**
- Identify differentiated capabilities (if sentiment is positive)
- Find product-specific issues (if sentiment is negative)
- Understand unique positioning

#### 🔗 Shared Topics
- Topics mentioned in both products' reviews
- Shows sentiment differences for the same topic
- Highlights where products compete directly

**Strategic Value:**
- See where you win (better sentiment on shared topics)
- Identify gaps (worse sentiment on shared topics)
- Understand competitive battlegrounds

#### ⚔️ Competitor Only Topics
- Topics mentioned exclusively in competitor's reviews
- Shows what customers emphasize for competitors

**Strategic Value:**
- Discover potential blind spots
- Identify emerging market needs
- Learn from competitor strengths

### **Sentiment Analysis**

For shared topics, the tool shows:
- **Sentiment scores** for both products (1-5 scale)
- **Mention counts** for context
- **Visual indicators**: ✅ (advantage), ⚠️ (gap), ➡️ (neutral)

### **Wins & Losses Breakdown**

#### ✅ Where You Win (Stronger Sentiment)
- Topics where your primary product has >0.2 points better sentiment
- Shows competitive advantages to leverage
- Provides data for sales enablement

#### ⚠️ Where You Trail (Weaker Sentiment)
- Topics where competitor has >0.2 points better sentiment
- Highlights areas needing improvement
- Identifies competitive vulnerabilities

### **Strategic Implications**

The tool automatically generates actionable insights:

#### 💡 Opportunities
1. **Learn from competitor's unique topics** - Understand what competitors focus on
2. **Close sentiment gaps** - Prioritize improvements on topics where competitors excel
3. **Address unique pain points** - Fix product-specific issues with low sentiment

#### 💪 Leverage Points
1. **Unique strengths** - Topics only your product excels at (high sentiment)
2. **Competitive advantages** - Shared topics where you win on sentiment
3. **Proven strengths** - Frequently mentioned topics with strong sentiment

## Example Use Cases

## Interpreting Results

### **High Topic Overlap (>70%)**
- Products are discussing similar themes
- Direct competition on same attributes
- Differentiation may be in execution quality (sentiment) not features

### **Low Topic Overlap (<30%)**
- Products serve different needs/markets
- Different positioning strategies
- Less direct competition

### **Sentiment Advantages (+0.5 or more)**
- Significant competitive strength
- Customers clearly prefer this aspect
- High confidence for competitive claims

### **Sentiment Gaps (-0.5 or more)**
- Critical weakness vs. competitor
- High priority for improvement
- Risk in competitive situations

### **Unique Topics with High Volume**
- If YOUR product: potential differentiated strength
- If COMPETITOR: possible gap or different market position

### **For Marketing**

### **For Customer Success**

- Leverage unique strengths in renewal conversations

## Data Considerations

### **Review Volume Matters**
- Comparisons with <10 reviews per product may be unreliable
- Higher volume = more statistical confidence
- Consider timeframe filters for recency

### **Sentiment Threshold**
- 0.2+ difference is notable but may be noise
- 0.5+ difference is significant and actionable
- 1.0+ difference is critical (rare but important)

### **Topic Mentions**
- Topics with <3 mentions may be outliers
- Focus on topics with 5+ mentions for reliability
- High-mention topics (10+) are most significant

## Quick Decision Framework

**When comparing Dayforce to a competitor:**

1. **Start with Shared Topics** - These are the battlegrounds
   - Wins = Sales ammunition
   - Losses = Product/CS priorities

2. **Review Unique Topics** - These show differentiation
   - High sentiment = Marketing opportunities
   - Low sentiment = Product risks

3. **Check Overlap %** - This shows market dynamics
   - High = direct competition, sentiment matters
   - Low = different positioning, features matter

4. **Prioritize by Volume** - Focus where customers talk most
   - High mention + positive = amplify
   - High mention + negative = fix urgently

5. **Validate with Examples** - Always check actual reviews
   - Sentiment scores are directional
   - Real quotes provide context and nuance
